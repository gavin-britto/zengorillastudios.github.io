Zen Gorilla Studios

Zen Gorilla Studios is a
brand created and led
by artist Gavin Britto.

At the center of this brand, is a fictional character, Zen Gorilla, who embodies the calmness of a Zen monk and the intensity of a gorilla. Depending on what the situation calls for, he is able to switch between these two ways of conducting himself, allowing for peace of mind and incredible craftsmanship.
